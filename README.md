# E-Commerce Customer & Sales Analytics

## Project Overview

This project analyzes e-commerce sales, customer segments, product performance, profitability, and shipping operations using SQL Server, Power BI, and Excel.

The goal is to transform raw transaction data into meaningful business insights that can support data-driven decision-making.

## Tools & Technologies

* Microsoft Excel: Data cleaning and preparation.
* SQL Server: Data querying, aggregation, and analysis.
* Power BI: Interactive dashboard and KPI visualization.

## Data Preparation

The dataset was cleaned and prepared before analysis. The cleaned dataset was saved in CSV format and imported for analysis.

## SQL Analysis

SQL queries were developed to analyze:

1. Sales by Category: Total orders, revenue, profit, and average order value.
2. Top 10 Products: Product sales, quantities sold, and profit.
3. Customer Segmentation: Customer counts, orders, and revenue by customer segment and region.
4. Shipping Performance: Delivery status, shipping type, and average actual shipping days.
5. Overall Profitability: Total revenue, total profit, and profit margin.

## Power BI Dashboard

The dashboard summarizes key business performance indicators and provides insights into sales, quantities, and profitability.

## Key Results

* Total Revenue: 10.26M
* Total Profit: 1.20M
* Profit Margin: 11.68%
* Distinct Orders: Approximately 48K

The revenue, profit, and profit margin results were checked against the SQL analysis.

## Business Value

The analysis can help businesses:

* Identify high-revenue product categories and products.
* Understand customer segments and regional performance.
* Monitor profitability and sales performance.
* Evaluate shipping operations and delivery status.
* Support data-driven business decisions.
